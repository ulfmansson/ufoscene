# Contributing to ufoscene.net

Thank you for wanting to help! ufoscene.net is a small hobby project, a hymn to the Swedish
demo scene, and the best way to contribute is with **ideas, not code**.

## What helps the most

Open an issue at <https://github.com/ulfmansson/ufoscene/issues/new/choose> with:

- **Feature requests:** something you would like the demos or the page to do.
- **Effect and demo ideas:** a classic demo effect, an 8-bit scene, a palette or a transition
  you remember from the Amiga, C64 or Spectrum days. A description is enough; a link to a video
  of the *technique* helps too.
- **Music:** songs for the playlists in [`web/playlists/`](web/playlists/), or internet radio
  stations that play well with the demos (for example ones that send the song name).
- **Scroll text lines:** greetings, jokes or demo scene memories, in Swedish or English.
- **Bugs:** something that looks broken, stutters or does not work in your browser or on your TV.
  Say which browser, which computer or TV, and what you did.
- **Party reports:** you showed it at a party? Tell us how it went. That is the best feedback of all.

Please be kind and keep it about the project.

## Why no code?

Pull requests with code are not accepted. This is a personal project with a particular look and
feel, and it is easier to keep it that way when the code comes from one place. Pull requests
will be closed with a friendly pointer to the issues, so open an issue with your idea instead;
good ideas get built.

You are of course welcome to fork the project and do anything you like with your copy: it is
[MIT licensed](LICENSE).

## Originality

Everything in UFO is original: graphics, sprites, scenes, the font and all texts. Classic demo
scene *techniques* are the point, but we do not recreate existing demos, games, logos or
characters, so please don't ask for a copy of a specific demo or game. "An effect like the
rotating checkerboards in 90s Amiga demos" is perfect; "the intro from <named demo>" is not.

## Support

If you would like to support the project in another way, there is a coffee jar at
<https://ko-fi.com/ufoscene>, and sharing <https://ufoscene.net> with **#ufoscene** helps a lot.
